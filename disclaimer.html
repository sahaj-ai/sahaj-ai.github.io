4. Voice recordings stay on your device

Voice typing in Sahaj is performed entirely on your own computer using an AI model bundled inside the app. Your microphone audio is never uploaded to us or to any third party. However, this means the accuracy of voice transcription depends on your microphone, background noise, and how clearly you speak. Please speak in a quiet environment for the best results.

5. Live AI and translation require internet

Two features reach out to third-party services over the internet:

  • Live AI transliteration — uses Google Input Tools
  • English ↔ Assamese translation — uses the MyMemory public API, and is currently in Beta

Whatever text you type into those specific features is sent to those providers to fetch a result. Please do not type confidential, sensitive, or personal information into those specific features (such as names of clients, financial data, or private correspondence). Every other feature of Sahaj works entirely offline and stays on your device.

About the Beta status of translation: the translation boxes are still experimental. They work by sending your text to a free public translation API, and while they're genuinely helpful for many everyday phrases, they're not yet at the level of a professional translator. You may occasionally see awkward phrasing, missed nuance, or the wrong word for a specific context. If accuracy really matters for a document, please treat the translation as a draft and review it yourself before using it. The feature will continue to improve in future updates.
