Privacy Policy

Sahaj is built to keep your work private. Almost everything happens on your own computer. This page explains exactly what little data we do handle, and why.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

The short version: Your typing, voice recordings, translations, and saved text never leave your device. We only receive two things when you activate: an anonymous machine ID and your license key. That's it. We do not use analytics, telemetry, or tracking of any kind.

1. Who we are

সহজ-Sahaj is a desktop application developed and maintained by Nazmul Hussain ("we", "us", "our"), an independent developer based in Assam, India. This Privacy Policy explains how the app and this website handle your information.

2. What stays on your device

The following information is created and stored only on your computer. We cannot see it, access it, or recover it for you:

  • All text you type in the editor
  • All voice recordings (processed locally by an offline AI model)
  • All autosaved drafts and backup copies
  • Your spell-check custom dictionary
  • Your saved helper buttons
  • Your app settings (theme, font size, preferences)

These files are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Sahaj_v1_1 on Windows. You can open this folder at any time to see exactly what's there, and delete it at any time.

3. What we receive during activation

When you activate Sahaj with your license key, the app sends a request to our license server containing two pieces of information:

  • Your license key — the code you received after purchase
  • An anonymous machine ID — a scrambled code derived from your computer's hardware identifiers using a one-way cryptographic hash (SHA-256)

That's all. We do not receive your name, email address (unless you contacted us separately), IP address logs beyond what's required to complete the request, or any information about what you type.

The machine ID cannot be reversed to reveal your hardware details or your identity. Its only purpose is to bind the license to one computer, so that one key can't be shared across multiple devices.

4. What we do NOT collect

Sahaj does not have, and never will have:

  • Analytics or usage statistics
  • Crash reporting or telemetry
  • Advertising identifiers or trackers
  • Any access to your typed text, voice audio, saved documents, or clipboard
  • Background processes that "phone home"

5. Third-party services used inside the app

Two optional features inside Sahaj send text to public third-party services to work. Your license does not pay for these services — they are bonus features. Only text you actively type into those specific features is sent.

Live AI transliteration → Google Input Tools
When you use the "Live AI" mode, the English word you're transliterating is sent to Google's public Input Tools API to fetch suggestions. Google may log these requests according to their own privacy policy. Please don't type confidential information into Live AI.
Translation boxes → MyMemory API
When you use the English → Assamese or Assamese → English translation boxes, the text you typed is sent to MyMemory (a public translation API) to fetch a result. Please don't translate confidential or sensitive text.

All other Sahaj features — Phonetic typing (offline mode), Voice typing, Mouse typing, InScript, spell check, hover dictionary — work completely offline and never send data anywhere.

6. Website services

When you visit our website, a few external services are used to display it:

  • Google Fonts — to load the Inter and Noto Sans Bengali typefaces
  • Blogger / Google image hosting — to load screenshots and the favicon
  • Razorpay — the checkout page you reach when you click "Get It Now"
  • Formspree — the suggestion/contact form on the homepage

These services operate under their own privacy policies. We only see what they forward to us — for example, Formspree forwards the name, email, and message you type into the suggestion form. Razorpay forwards us the payment confirmation and your email address, so we can send your license key.

7. Cookies

Our own website does not set any cookies. Razorpay's checkout page may set cookies when you're completing a purchase, but those are set by Razorpay and governed by their policy. The Sahaj desktop app itself does not use cookies at all.

8. How long we keep data

Activation records (license key + machine ID + email) are kept for as long as your license is active, plus a short period afterward for support purposes. Suggestion form submissions are deleted once we've read and acted on them. If you ask us to delete your data, we will do so within 30 days, except where we're required by law to keep records (for example, payment receipts for tax purposes).

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask what data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete your data
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent for future processing (which may mean we can't continue to support your license)

To exercise any of these rights, email hussainnazmul786@gmail.com. We respond to all genuine requests within 30 days.

10. Data security

Your license key is stored on your own computer (in the Windows registry and a local file), and on our server as a record of activation. We use HTTPS for all server communication and Ed25519 cryptographic signatures on license tokens to prevent tampering. While no system is perfectly secure, we take reasonable measures to protect the small amount of data we hold.

11. Children's privacy

Sahaj is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we'll delete it.

12. International users

Our license server may process data outside India (for example, on cloud infrastructure located in other countries). By using Sahaj, you consent to this transfer of your minimal activation data. If you are in the EU/UK, you have additional rights under GDPR which we honour on a best-effort basis — please contact us if you have specific questions.

13. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect new features or legal requirements. The current version will always be on this page, with a "Last updated" date. Continuing to use Sahaj after an update means you accept the updated policy.
